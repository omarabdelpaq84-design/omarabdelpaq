Services

Practical, end-to-end data work — from a messy spreadsheet to a dashboard your team actually opens every day.

Power BI Dashboard Development

I build interactive Power BI dashboards with DAX measures, drill-downs, and slicers — designed around the 3-5 questions leadership actually asks every week, so you get a single source of truth your team opens daily instead of a report nobody reads twice.

SQL & Database Analysis

I write query logic, joins, and aggregations against PostgreSQL and relational data to answer specific business questions fast — pulling answers straight from the source instead of guesswork, whether it's a one-off report or a recurring analysis.

Python Data Cleaning & ETL

I build Pandas-based pipelines that take raw, inconsistent files and load them into a clean, analysis-ready structure — repeatable, not a one-off script, so your data stays trustworthy without re-checking it every time.

Excel Power Pivot & BI Reporting

I design star-schema data models, DAX measures, and PivotChart dashboards for teams that live in Excel and need it to do more — self-service reporting your team can update on its own without waiting on me.
